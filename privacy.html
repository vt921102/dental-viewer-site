Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Dental Viewer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your Mac.

What the app does with your files

  • Dental Viewer opens only the files and folders you choose — through the Import menu, by dragging them onto the window, or with Open With in Finder. macOS's App Sandbox prevents it from reading anything else.
  • Files are read on your Mac and shown on your Mac. They are never uploaded, synced or sent anywhere.
  • The app never modifies the files or folders you open.
  • To show a zip export, the app extracts a temporary copy inside its own private container on your Mac. That copy is deleted when you open another case or quit the app.
  • Screenshots you save with Save Screenshot are written only to the location you pick.

No network access

Dental Viewer has no permission to connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party services that could receive your data.

No accounts

There is no sign-in and no account. The app does not know who you are.

Patient information

Dental exports can contain patient details such as names, dates of birth and order numbers. Dental Viewer only displays these on your screen; it does not record, transmit or retain them. You remain responsible for handling patient data in line with the rules that apply to your practice, such as HIPAA or the GDPR.

Purchases

If you buy Dental Viewer, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment or contact details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Dental Viewer is intended for dental professionals, laboratories and students. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change will never introduce data collection without being stated here first.
