Dental Viewer

Open intraoral scans in 3D on your Mac.

Dental Viewer opens the files your intraoral scanner or dental CAD software exports and shows them in an interactive 3D view — no vendor software, no account, no upload. Everything stays on your Mac.

  • STL, PLY, OBJ, 3MF, glTF and VTK meshes, plus CBCT and DICOM
  • Zip exports, case folders and nested archives
  • Case details, snapshots and per-mesh measurements
  • Apple Silicon and Intel · macOS 14 or later
